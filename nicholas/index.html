How I build.

I built both apps in SwiftUI and SwiftData using a workflow where larger Claude models plan and review work implemented by smaller GPT models. The work moves through implementation, testing, and revision.

I’m learning to keep that process focused: fewer active projects, clearer responsibilities, and less time coordinating work that doesn’t help solve the problem in front of me.

Music, school, and family.

At Middlebury, I’m the Assistant Business Manager and Social Media Manager for the Dissipated Eight, our a cappella group. I also tutor my brothers and help with family caregiving. Those responsibilities shape what I choose to build.
