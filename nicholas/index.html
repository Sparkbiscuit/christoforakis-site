The promise

A schedule with a sense of now.

Loom is a time-aware ADHD scheduling app. The question behind it is concrete: what should I be doing this minute?

A day is not a static checklist. Time moves, commitments stay put, plans change, and the useful answer has to change with them.

Loom is meant to make that answer calm and honest—without turning opening the app into another decision.
