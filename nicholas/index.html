Filuma

A plan has to survive the day.

Filuma turns a task, a deadline and a rough estimate of effort into blocks of work around the rest of a day, with ADHD planning challenges in mind. When the day slips it replans, earlier deadlines first. If the work no longer fits, it says so, instead of quietly missing the deadline.

Starting is the one step a plan can’t take for you, so Filuma tries to make it smaller: an optional first step rides along on the Focus card, the session timer and the notification.

Meds Ahead

What runs out next, and why.

Meds Ahead began with my family’s need to coordinate medications and refills. Supply comes from what you record, refills, corrections and doses, so every run-out date has a “Why this date?” view that shows its working.

Nothing leaves your iPhone: no account, no ads, no third-party analytics.

An organization tool, not medical advice. The screens use sample medications.

Aloft

It doesn’t solve a problem close to home, unless a short runway counts.

A flight game that runs in a browser: island hopping in airliners, from a mainland hub out to the kinds of runways pilots tell stories about. It runs on a phone or a computer and installs like an app. The fleet includes the Embraer E190, a folding-wing 777X and Concorde.

Say hello
