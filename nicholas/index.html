01 / Filuma · iPhone & iPad

A plan you can
actually start.

Filuma is my ADHD-focused scheduling app, previously called Loom. Add a task, a deadline, and a rough effort estimate. It turns that work into manageable blocks around the parts of your day that are already spoken for.

The Focus view brings the next block and its first step to the front. Work sessions track the time you put in, and the plan adjusts when the day changes.

The aim is simple: spend less energy deciding what to do, and make it easier to begin.

02 / Meds Ahead · iPhone

Know what
runs out next.

I built Meds Ahead because my family needed help coordinating medications and refills. Reminders were only part of the problem. We also needed to know what was running low, and when to act.

Meds Ahead brings today’s doses, medication records, and supply forecasts together. Log a dose, add a refill, or correct a count, and the forecast updates.

Label scanning happens on the iPhone, with results you review before saving. Medication records are stored locally, with no account required.

Behind the apps

How I build.

I built both apps in SwiftUI and SwiftData using a workflow where larger Claude models plan and review work implemented by smaller GPT models. The work moves through implementation, testing, and revision.

I’m learning to keep that process focused: fewer active projects, clearer responsibilities, and less time coordinating work that doesn’t help solve the problem in front of me.
