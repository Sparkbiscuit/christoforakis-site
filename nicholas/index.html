Filuma

iPhone app · on the App Store

A planner for people with ADHD, which is to say a planner for me. You give it a task, a deadline and a rough guess at the effort, and it breaks the work into blocks that fit around the rest of your day. When the day slips, it replans. When the work won’t fit anymore, it tells you, instead of letting the deadline sneak up on you.

Starting is the hard part, so it keeps the first step in front of you: on the Focus card, in the timer and in the notification.

Built with SwiftUI, SwiftData and EventKit.

Meds Ahead

iPhone app · free, no account

My family keeps track of a lot of medications. Remembering doses was never really the problem. Knowing which bottle was about to run out was. Meds Ahead works out what runs out next from the doses, refills and counts you log, and every date has a “Why this date?” view that shows the math.

Nothing leaves your phone: no account, no ads, no analytics.

Built with SwiftUI, SwiftData and Vision. The screen here uses sample medications. It’s an organization tool, not medical advice.

Aloft

Flight game · runs in a browser

Island hopping in airliners, out to the kinds of runways pilots tell stories about: Lukla, St Barth, Skiathos, Gibraltar and Kai Tak’s checkerboard. The fleet has an Embraer E190, a folding-wing 777X and Concorde. It runs on a phone or a computer and installs like an app.

It doesn’t solve a problem close to home, unless a short runway counts.
