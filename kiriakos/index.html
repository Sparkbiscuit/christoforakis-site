Mr. Pizza House.

Kiriakos runs Mr. Pizza House on Blue Hills Avenue in Hartford. The menu spans pizza, grinders, salads, and more, with pickup and delivery available through the restaurant’s website.

A menu shaped by the community.

In June 2022, WFSB featured Kiriakos and Mr. Pizza in a collaboration with UConn Health and Hartford community members to develop healthier menu options. He shared a barbecue chicken pizza made with a lower-sodium sauce.
